About

deGron

I am a native Japanese speaker working on English-to-Japanese game localization.

I work on Japanese localization as a volunteer to help indie games reach Japanese players, especially games that are not yet available in Japanese.

Rather than simply replacing English text with Japanese, I focus on making the localization read naturally in-game while preserving each character's voice and the overall tone of the game.

For Developers

For Developers

If you are considering Japanese localization for your game, feel free to get in touch.

Send me some information about your game and what you would like help with. I will take a look at the game and the scope of the localization. If it is a project I would like to help with and the scope is something I can reasonably handle, I can help with the Japanese localization on a volunteer basis.

During translation, I may ask about context, character details, or other information that cannot be determined from the source text alone when necessary.

Whenever possible, I would also like to check the translated Japanese text in the game itself to make sure there are no problems with context, text display, UI, or other localization-related elements.

Translation

  • Dialogue, narrative text, and narration
  • UI text
  • Items, skills, descriptions, and similar text
  • Steam store pages and other game-related text
  • Terminology and character voice consistency

Translation Review & Editing

  • Reviewing and editing existing Japanese translations
  • Checking Japanese for naturalness and contextual accuracy
  • Checking terminology and character voice consistency
  • Reviewing and editing Japanese produced using machine translation or AI translation

Implementation & Localization QA (LQA)

  • Implementing translated text into the game
  • Selecting and adjusting Japanese fonts
  • Checking Japanese text in-game
  • Identifying and fixing UI issues and other localization-related problems
Volunteer Localization

About My Volunteer Localization

This is a personal volunteer activity, not a commercial translation service.

I genuinely love games and want to support the people who make them. If I can help a game I care about reach more players in Japan, I'm happy to contribute through Japanese localization.

For games that I have reviewed and would like to help localize, I do not charge a localization fee.

Commercial games are welcome. The game does not need to be distributed on Steam, and I am open to both games currently in development and games that have already been released.

If your game already has a Japanese localization, I may also be able to help review and improve the existing translation.

I do not limit myself to any particular genre. However, I cannot accept every project or request.

Credit is optional.
